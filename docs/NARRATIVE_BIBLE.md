# Narrative Bible

The story is drawn from the creator's own life: growing up, and the friends who went on to start the band Being As An Ocean.

**Status: open.** Nothing below is canon until the creator fills it in.

## Ground rules
- Real people appear only with their OK. Anyone who hasn't agreed gets a fictional name and likeness.
- Real band recordings and the band name need sign-off from the other members and the label before they ship. Until then, placeholder music goes through the `SongDef` data.
- Recovery, family hardship and faith are treated honestly. Nothing is glamorized.

## Open questions for the creator
1. Does the protagonist stay "Cub", or use your real name or nickname?
2. Hometown and era: which town, which years, and which real spots (skate spots, venues, the rehearsal garage)?
3. Who are the core friends, and do they each want to be in it?
4. What do you play, and what did the band sound like before it found itself?
5. Which real moments are the chapter beats? (For example: the first show, a name change, signing, the first tour.)
6. How should leaving the band, and what came after, figure in the arc?
7. Tone: playful, dramatic, or both?
